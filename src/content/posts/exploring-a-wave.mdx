A sine wave is one of the simplest models of an oscillation. Its frequency tells
us how many complete cycles happen each second.

For a wave with unit amplitude and zero initial phase:

**y(t) = sin(2πft)**

Here, **t** is time in seconds and **f** is frequency in hertz.

## Try it

Move the slider to change the frequency. The horizontal axis always covers two
seconds, so a wave at 1 Hz completes two cycles; at 2 Hz, it completes four.

<SineWave />

Notice that the height of the wave stays the same. Increasing the frequency
changes how often it repeats, independently of its amplitude.
